**The common thread? A massive, costly, and emotionally draining communication gap.**

**InterpWhat is the "Google Translate" for non-verbal communication.**

Our first product, **InterpPet**, is a live, functional web application that helps pet owners and shelters understand animal behavior, improve welfare, and strengthen the human-animal bond.

## Slide 7: Go-To-Market Strategy

**A Three-Pronged Approach to Dominate the Pet Market**

1.  **Direct-to-Consumer (D2C):**
    *   Freemium SaaS model with two subscription tiers:
        *   **Essential:** $14.99/mo
        *   **Premium:** $29.99/mo
    *   Targeted content marketing and community building.

2.  **Shelter Partnerships (B2B2C):**
    *   **$33/mo** subscription for shelters.
    *   Provides tools to improve animal welfare and reduce returns.
    *   **10% revenue share** from referred adopters, creating a viral loop.

3.  **Affiliate Marketing:**
    *   **25% recurring commission** for 12 months.
    *   Targeting pet influencers, bloggers, and related businesses.
