## 🎯 The Opportunity in 60 Seconds

**The Problem:** 85 million pet-owning households struggle to understand their pets' behavior, leading to stress, anxiety, and preventable vet visits.

**Our Solution:** InterpWhat is an AI-powered platform that analyzes pet photos, videos, and sounds to provide instant behavioral insights and personalized care recommendations.

**The Market:** A $147 billion pet industry with a $2.8 billion (and growing) digital pet products segment.

**The Traction:** Fully functional product, 5 personalized shelter outreach emails ready to send, complete marketing package, and a mission-driven story that resonates.

**The Ask:** $500K - $1M to fund mobile app development, customer acquisition, and our first 18 months of operations.

**The Vision:** Become the market leader in applied interpretive AI, starting with pets and expanding into elderly care, baby tech, and beyond.
