# PRD Research Findings

## What Should a PRD Include (Atlassian Best Practices)

1. **Project Specifics**
   - Participants (product owner, team, stakeholders)
   - Status (on target, at risk, delayed, etc.)
   - Target release date

2. **Team Goals and Business Objectives**
   - Clear, concise statement of purpose

3. **Background and Strategic Fit**
   - Why are we doing this?
   - How does this fit into overall company objectives?

4. **Assumptions**
   - Technical, business, or user assumptions

5. **User Stories**
   - Detailed user stories with success metrics
   - Links to customer interviews
   - Screenshots and evidence

6. **User Interaction and Design**
   - Design explorations and wireframes

7. **Questions**
   - Table of "things we need to decide or research"

8. **What We're Not Doing**
   - Out of scope items
   - Future considerations

## Common PRD Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Lack of Clarity and Specificity**
   - Avoid vague language
   - Be specific about features and requirements
   - Example: Instead of "user-friendly," say "simple, intuitive interface that allows users to complete tasks easily"

2. **Overcomplicating the PRD**
   - Keep focused on critical features
   - Don't overwhelm the development team
   - Example: Simplify "AI-powered chatbot with NLP" to "chatbot feature that provides helpful recommendations"

3. **Failing to Prioritize Features**
   - Rank features by importance
   - Focus resources on what matters most
   - Example: "Mobile app is critical, followed by web app and desktop app"

4. **Ignoring User Needs and Feedback**
   - Research and gather user feedback
   - Inform PRD with actual user data
   - Example: "User research indicates users want to share on Instagram and Twitter" vs generic "social media"

5. **Not Considering Technical Limitations**
   - Ensure PRD is feasible with available resources
   - Consider development team capabilities
   - Example: Specify "Windows, Mac OS, and Linux" instead of "all major operating systems"

## Additional Best Practices

- **Define Success Metrics**: Include measurable KPIs (user engagement, conversion rates, customer satisfaction)
- **Use Visual Aids**: Include wireframes, flowcharts, diagrams
- **Collaborate with Developers**: Ensure vision is feasible and achievable
- **One Page, One Source**: Keep it simple and accessible
- **Living Document**: Link to related issues, maintain real-time updates
- **Separate Problem from Solution**: Business context and problem statements should be distinct
- **Include Constraints**: Budget, timeline, technical limitations
