## 📋 Overview

InterpWhat has evolved from a credit-based behavior analysis tool into a comprehensive, subscription-based pet parenting platform. This document summarizes all updated marketing materials that reflect this transformation.

---

## 🆕 What Changed

### Product Evolution

InterpWhat has added three major premium features that transform the app from a single-purpose tool into an all-in-one pet parent toolkit:

1. **AI Breed Identification** - Instantly discover your pet's breed mix from a photo
2. **Care Reminders & Notifications** - Never miss medications, vet appointments, or grooming sessions
3. **Multi-Pet Comparison** - Compare behaviors and timelines across multiple pets

### Business Model Shift

We have transitioned from a credit-based pricing model to a subscription model with three tiers:

*   **Essential ($9.99/mo):** 10 monthly analyses, Pet Voice™, Pet Timeline™
*   **Premium ($19.99/mo):** 30 monthly analyses + all new features (Breed ID, Reminders, Comparison)
*   **Shelter Partner ($99/3mo):** Unlimited analyses for registered shelters and rescues
