## 🎯 What is Phase 3?

Phase 3 is about **real-world usage**. We want you to use InterpWhat as you normally would - no special tasks, just your regular pet parenting routine.

**Duration:** 2 weeks  
**Expectation:** Use the app naturally and share your honest feedback

## 🐛 Reporting Bugs

When reporting a bug, please include:

1. **What you were doing** when the bug occurred
2. **What you expected** to happen
3. **What actually happened**
4. **Screenshots or videos** (if possible)
5. **Device/Browser** information

**Example:**
```
I was trying to upload a photo for analysis. After selecting the file, 
the upload button became unresponsive. I expected the photo to upload 
and show a progress indicator. I'm using Chrome on Windows 10.
```

---

## 💡 Feature Requests

We love hearing your ideas! When suggesting features:

1. **Describe the feature** clearly
2. **Explain why** it would be helpful
3. **Give examples** of how you'd use it

**Example:**
```
I'd love to be able to export my pet's timeline as a PDF. This would 
help me share my pet's progress with my vet. I'd use it before annual 
checkups to show behavior changes over the year.
```

## ⭐ What Makes Great Feedback?

### Good Feedback
- ✅ Specific and actionable
- ✅ Explains the "why"
- ✅ Includes context
- ✅ Constructive and helpful

### Examples of Great Feedback

**Bug Report:**
> "When I try to upload a photo larger than 10MB, I get an error message, 
> but it doesn't tell me the file size limit. It would be helpful if 
> the error message said 'File must be under 10MB' so I know what to do."

**Feature Request:**
> "I'd love to see a dark mode option. I often check on my pets at night, 
> and the bright screen can be jarring. It would also help with battery 
> life on mobile devices."

**UX Feedback:**
> "The reminder creation form is a bit confusing. I wasn't sure if 
> 'Recurring' meant it repeats daily or weekly. Maybe add a tooltip or 
> example text to clarify."

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How often should I use the app?
**A:** Use it as naturally as possible. If you normally check on your pets daily, do that. If weekly, that's fine too. We want to see real-world usage patterns.

### Q: What if I find a critical bug?
**A:** Report it immediately via email (beta@interpwhat.com) or the feedback widget. Mark it as "Critical" in the category.

### Q: Can I invite friends?
**A:** Not yet - we're keeping Phase 3 limited to current beta testers. But we'd love referrals for our public launch!

### Q: Will my feedback be implemented?
**A:** We review all feedback and prioritize based on impact and feasibility. We'll implement the most valuable suggestions first.

### Q: What if I can't complete the surveys?
**A:** That's okay! We understand life gets busy. Just provide feedback when you can via the in-app widget.

### Q: Is my data secure?
**A:** Yes! We take data security seriously. Your pet photos and data are encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information.
