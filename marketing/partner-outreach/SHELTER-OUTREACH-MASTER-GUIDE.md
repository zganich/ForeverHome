## 💡 Tips for Success

### Do:
- ✅ Personalize every email (don't copy-paste)
- ✅ Follow up persistently but respectfully
- ✅ Offer free trials (removes risk)
- ✅ Emphasize local connections (Utah, Texas)
- ✅ Lead with their mission, not your product
- ✅ Be patient (shelters are busy, understaffed)

### Don't:
- ❌ Send generic emails
- ❌ Give up after one email
- ❌ Be pushy or salesy
- ❌ Talk only about features (talk about outcomes)
- ❌ Forget to follow up
- ❌ Expect immediate responses
