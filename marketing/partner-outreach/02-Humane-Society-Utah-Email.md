- **Care reminders** reduce missed vet appointments and medication doses
- **Timeline tracking** helps adopters monitor behavioral progress and catch issues early

**For Your Pet Retention Program:**
- Give struggling pet owners a tool to better understand their pet's behavior before considering surrender
- Provide behavior insights that complement your training programs
- Help families troubleshoot issues at home with AI-powered guidance

**For Your Foster Network:**
- Help foster families understand temporary pets' behaviors and needs
- Track behavioral changes during foster care
- Provide adopters with behavioral history and insights
