- **Timeline tracking** helps adopters monitor behavioral progress and celebrate milestones

**For Your Foster Network:**
- Help foster volunteers understand temporary pets' behaviors and needs
- Track behavioral changes during foster care
- Provide adopters with behavioral history and insights from foster period

**For Your Community Programs:**
- Complement your youth programming with tech-forward pet care education
- Support your community cat program with feline behavior insights
