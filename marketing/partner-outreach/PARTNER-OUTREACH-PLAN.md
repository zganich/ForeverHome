# InterpWhat Partner Outreach Plan

**Goal:** Sign 25 shelter partners, 20 influencer affiliates, and 10 vet practices in Year 1

---

## 🎯 Target Partner Profiles

### 1. Animal Shelters & Rescues

*   **Profile:** No-kill shelters, foster-based rescues, and humane societies with a strong community presence and 50+ adoptions per year.
*   **Value Prop:** Empower adopters, reduce returns, and create a new recurring donation stream.
*   **Offer:** Shelter Partner Plan ($99/3mo for unlimited use) + 10% revenue share on referred Premium subscriptions.

### 2. Pet Influencers

*   **Profile:** Micro-influencers (10K-100K followers) on Instagram & TikTok with high engagement rates and an authentic connection with their audience.
*   **Value Prop:** Share a genuinely useful and innovative tool with your followers and earn recurring income.
*   **Offer:** 20% lifetime commission on all referred Premium subscriptions.

### 3. Veterinary Practices

*   **Profile:** Modern, tech-savvy vet clinics focused on preventative care and client education.
*   **Value Prop:** Provide a valuable tool for clients to track behavior between visits and improve communication.
*   **Offer:** 15% revenue share on all client referrals to the Premium plan.

---

## 📧 Email Outreach Sequences

### Sequence 1: Animal Shelters

*   **Email 1 (Day 1):** Intro & Mission Alignment
    *   Subject: A new way to support [Shelter Name] adopters
    *   Content: Praise their work, introduce InterpWhat and Chantell's story, explain how our app can help their adopters succeed.
*   **Email 2 (Day 4):** The Problem We Solve
    *   Subject: Reduce return rates & empower new pet parents
    *   Content: Focus on the challenges new adopters face and how InterpWhat provides the support they need 24/7.
*   **Email 3 (Day 10):** The Partnership Offer
    *   Subject: A partnership that supports your mission (and your budget)
    *   Content: Detail the Shelter Partner Plan and the 10% revenue share. Propose a brief 15-minute call.
*   **Email 4 (Day 17):** Case Study & Final Follow-Up
    *   Subject: How [Similar Shelter] is using InterpWhat
    *   Content: Share a (hypothetical for now) case study. One last attempt to connect.

### Sequence 2: Pet Influencers

*   **Email 1 (Day 1):** Authentic Partnership Opportunity
    *   Subject: Your followers will love this, [Influencer Name]
    *   Content: Compliment their content, introduce InterpWhat and Pet Voice™, and explain why it would be a perfect fit for their audience.
*   **Email 2 (Day 3):** The Affiliate Offer
    *   Subject: Earn recurring revenue with InterpWhat
    *   Content: Detail the 20% lifetime commission. Explain how easy it is to get started. Offer a free lifetime Premium account.
*   **Email 3 (Day 7):** Content Ideas & Follow-Up
    *   Subject: 3 content ideas for your channel
    *   Content: Suggest specific, creative ways they could feature InterpWhat. Ask if they have any questions.

### Sequence 3: Veterinary Practices

*   **Email 1 (Day 1):** A Tool to Enhance Client Communication
    *   Subject: A new tool for your clients, [Practice Name]
    *   Content: Introduce InterpWhat as a way for clients to better track and report behavioral issues between visits.
*   **Email 2 (Day 5):** The Value for Your Practice
    *   Subject: Improve client outcomes & add a new revenue stream
    *   Content: Explain how InterpWhat can lead to more informed clients and detail the 15% revenue share offer.
*   **Email 3 (Day 12):** Demo & Discussion
    *   Subject: Quick 15-min demo of InterpWhat?
    *   Content: Offer a brief, no-obligation demo to show them how the platform works and answer any questions.

## 🛠️ Outreach Toolkit

*   **CRM:** Use a simple spreadsheet or a tool like HubSpot CRM (free tier) to track outreach.
*   **Templates:** Use the email sequences above as templates, but personalize each one.
*   **Pitch Deck:** Attach the `InterpWhat-Partner-Pitch-Deck-v3.md` to relevant emails.
*   **Scheduling:** Use Calendly or a similar tool to make booking demos easy.

## 🚀 Execution Plan (First 30 Days)

*   **Week 1:** Identify and build a list of 50 target shelters, 50 pet influencers, and 20 vet practices.
*   **Week 2:** Begin sending Email 1 of each sequence. Track opens and replies.
*   **Week 3:** Send follow-up emails. Schedule and conduct initial discovery calls and demos.
*   **Week 4:** Continue follow-ups. Aim to sign the first 2-3 partners in each category.
