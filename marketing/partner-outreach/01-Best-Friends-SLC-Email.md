**That's why we built InterpWhat.**

InterpWhat is an AI-powered pet behavior analysis platform that helps new pet parents understand their dogs and cats better. We're reaching out to Best Friends because we believe our tool could significantly support your adopters—especially those bringing home rescue animals who may have unknown histories or behavioral challenges.

### How InterpWhat Helps Your Adopters

- **Instant Behavior Insights:** Upload a photo or video, get AI-powered analysis in 15-25 seconds
- **Breed Identification:** Perfect for mixed-breed rescues with unknown backgrounds  
- **Care Reminders:** Help adopters stay on top of vet appointments, medications, and training schedules
- **Multi-Pet Support:** Compare behaviors for households adopting multiple animals
- **Timeline Tracking:** Monitor behavioral progress over time to catch issues early

### Why This Matters for NKUT

You're taking in 2,500+ animals per year through your foster-based model. Many of these pets have experienced trauma, uncertainty, or behavioral challenges. InterpWhat gives adopters the confidence and tools they need to succeed—which means fewer returns, better outcomes, and stronger bonds.
