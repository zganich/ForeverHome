# InterpWhat - Sales One-Pager
## The All-In-One App for Modern Pet Parents

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Modern pet ownership is complex. Owners are juggling vet appointments, medication schedules, behavioral issues, and the desire to provide the best possible care. They need more than just a single tool; they need an integrated system that helps them **understand, care for, and manage** their pet's entire life, from decoding their behavior to remembering their flea medication.

---

## 💡 The Solution

**InterpWhat is the all-in-one pet parenting app that combines AI-powered behavior analysis with essential care management tools.**

We provide a complete toolkit for pet owners to understand their pets' needs, stay organized, and build a deeper bond. Our platform offers instant insights, automated reminders, and powerful comparison features, making proactive and informed pet care easier than ever.

---

## ✨ Key Features: The Complete Pet Parent Toolkit

| Feature | Description |

| **🎤 Pet Voice™** | The viral feature that lets you hear what your pet is thinking in one of 7 fun personality voices. |
