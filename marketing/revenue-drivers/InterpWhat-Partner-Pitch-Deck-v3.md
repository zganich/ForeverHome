## Slide 2: The Problem

# Modern Pet Parenting is Overwhelming

Pet owners are drowning in a sea of single-purpose apps, conflicting online advice, and manual tracking methods. They are juggling:

*   **Behavioral Mysteries:** What does that bark *really* mean?
*   **Health Worries:** Is this normal, or should I call the vet?
*   **Care Logistics:** Remembering medications, appointments, and grooming.
*   **Multiple Pets:** Trying to manage the unique needs of each animal.

**The result is stress, anxiety, and missed opportunities to provide the best care.**

## Slide 3: The Solution

# InterpWhat: The All-in-One Pet Parent Toolkit

InterpWhat is the first app to integrate AI-powered behavioral insights with a complete suite of care management tools. We empower pet owners to:

*   **Understand** their pet's behavior with AI analysis.
*   **Care** for their pet's health with reminders and tracking.
*   **Manage** the complexities of a multi-pet household.

**It's the single source of truth for a happier, healthier pet.**
