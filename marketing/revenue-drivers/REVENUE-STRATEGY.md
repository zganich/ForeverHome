## 💰 Revenue Streams

### Primary Revenue: Direct Subscriptions

InterpWhat generates revenue through three subscription tiers:

| Tier | Price | Target Audience | Monthly Revenue per 100 Users |
|------|-------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| **Essential** | $9.99/mo | Budget-conscious pet owners | $999 |
| **Premium** | $19.99/mo | Dedicated pet parents (PRIMARY TARGET) | $1,999 |
| **Shelter Partner** | $99/3mo ($33/mo) | Shelters & rescues | $3,300 |

**Primary Focus:** Premium tier drives the highest revenue per user and showcases all features.

---

### Secondary Revenue: Partnership Commissions

*   **Influencer Affiliates:** 20% commission on referred subscriptions
*   **Veterinary Practices:** 15% revenue share on client referrals

## 🤝 Partnership Revenue Strategy

### Partner Types & Revenue Impact

#### 1. Animal Shelters (HIGH IMPACT)

**Revenue Model:** Shelter Partner subscriptions ($99/3 months = $33/mo per shelter)

**Volume Potential:** 100 shelters in Year 1 = **$3,300 MRR** = **$39,600 ARR**

**Additional Value:**
*   Each shelter promotes InterpWhat to 50-200 adopters
*   Estimated 10% conversion = 5-20 new Premium subs per shelter
*   100 shelters × 10 subs = 1,000 new Premium users = **$239,880 ARR**

**Total Shelter Impact:** ~$280K ARR potential

---

#### 2. Pet Influencers (MEDIUM-HIGH IMPACT)

**Revenue Model:** 20% affiliate commission on referred subscriptions

**Volume Potential:** 20 micro-influencers (10K-50K followers each)

**Assumptions:**
*   Each influencer drives 50 Premium annual subs
*   20 influencers × 50 subs = 1,000 annual subs = **$119,940 ARR**
*   Influencer commission: 20% = **$23,988 cost**
*   Net revenue: **$95,952**

---

#### 3. Veterinary Practices (MEDIUM IMPACT)

**Revenue Model:** 15% revenue share on client referrals

**Volume Potential:** 10 vet practices in Year 1

**Assumptions:**
*   Each practice has 500 active clients
*   5% adoption rate = 25 new Premium subs per practice
*   10 practices × 25 subs = 250 annual subs = **$29,985 ARR**
*   Vet commission: 15% = **$4,498 cost**
*   Net revenue: **$25,487**

---

#### 4. Pet Brands (LOW-MEDIUM IMPACT)

**Revenue Model:** Sponsored content and co-marketing campaigns

**Volume Potential:** 3-5 brand partnerships in Year 1

**Revenue:**
*   Sponsored social posts: $500-$2,000 per campaign
*   Co-branded content: $1,000-$5,000 per partnership
*   Product placement in app: $2,000-$10,000 per quarter

**Estimated Annual Revenue:** **$15,000-$50,000**
