## 🎯 Executive Summary

This playbook outlines InterpWhat's Black Friday 2025 strategy, rebuilt to support our new subscription model and feature set, including **Breed Identification, Care Reminders, and Multi-Pet Comparison**. We are shifting from a one-time credit bundle to a high-value annual subscription offer. This campaign is designed to drive significant recurring revenue and establish InterpWhat as the indispensable all-in-one pet parenting platform.

**Key Updates in v3.0:**
- **Offer Overhaul:** From a one-time credit bundle to a heavily discounted **Annual Premium Subscription**.
- **New Feature Showcase:** Marketing will be centered around the value of our new premium features.
- **Updated Messaging:** Focused on becoming the ultimate pet parent with a complete toolkit.
- **Revised Financial Goals:** Metrics are now focused on Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) and customer lifetime value (LTV).

## 🚨 Risk Mitigation

*   **Risk:** Users are resistant to a subscription offer after being accustomed to credits.
*   **Mitigation:** Clearly communicate the immense value of the annual plan (360 analyses + premium features for the price of ~120 credits). Offer a monthly subscription option on the page as a fallback.
*   **Risk:** Technical issues with the subscription checkout.
*   **Mitigation:** Thoroughly test the Stripe subscription flow before launch.
