# InterpWhat 30-Day Social Media Calendar v3.0
## The All-in-One Pet Parent Toolkit

**Updated:** October 16, 2025
**Platforms:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter/X
**Focus:** Driving Premium subscriptions by showcasing the full suite of new features.

---

## 📊 Content Strategy Overview

### Content Pillars (v3)

*   **50% Feature-Benefit:** Directly showcasing how Breed ID, Reminders, and Comparison solve real pet owner problems.
*   **30% Educational:** Broader pet care topics that lead back to the app's value.
*   **10% Promotional:** Direct calls-to-action for the Premium subscription and Black Friday deal.
*   **10% Community:** User-generated content and engagement.

---

## 🎯 Week 1: Introducing the All-In-One Toolkit

### Day 1: The Evolution of InterpWhat

*   **Instagram/TikTok (Reel):** A fast-paced montage showing the app's evolution from a simple analyzer to a full toolkit. End with the new tagline: "Understand, Care, Compare."
*   **Facebook/Twitter:** Announce the new InterpWhat. "It's more than just hearing their voice. It's about understanding their world. Introducing the all-in-one pet parent app."

### Day 2: Feature Spotlight - What's Your Pet's Breed?

*   **Instagram/TikTok (Reel):** A user uploads a photo of their mixed-breed dog. The app analyzes it and reveals the top 3 breed matches. Text overlay: "Ever wonder what breed your rescue is? Now you can know."
*   **Hashtags:** #BreedID #DogDNA #RescueDog #PetTech

### Day 3: Educational - Why Breed Matters

*   **Instagram (Carousel):** "3 Reasons to Know Your Dog's Breed." 1. Breed-specific health risks. 2. Temperament and training style. 3. Exercise and dietary needs.
*   **Facebook:** A blog post expanding on the topic, with a CTA to try the Breed ID feature in the app.

### Day 4: Feature Spotlight - Never Forget a Vet Visit Again

*   **Instagram/TikTok (Reel):** A user gets a push notification: "Reminder: Luna's annual check-up is tomorrow!" The user smiles, relieved.
*   **Hashtags:** #PetCare #VetVisit #DogMeds #GoodPetParent

### Day 5: User Story - A Multi-Pet Household

*   **Instagram/TikTok (Reel):** A user shows the "Compare Pets" screen, with their two dogs' timelines side-by-side. Pet Voice clips play, showing their different personalities. "Finally, I can see why Max is so chill and Daisy is so... not."
*   **Hashtags:** #DogPack #MultiPetHome #DogFriends

### Day 6: The Premium Advantage

*   **Instagram (Story):** A poll: "What's your biggest pet care challenge?" with options like "Understanding behavior," "Remembering meds," "Juggling multiple pets." Follow up with slides showing how Premium solves each one.
*   **Facebook:** A post detailing the full list of features in the Essential vs. Premium plans.

### Day 7: Community Q&A

*   **Instagram Live/Facebook Live:** A Q&A session with Chantell, answering questions about the new features and the app's mission. Announce that the Black Friday deal will be revealed next week.




## 🎯 Week 2: Deep Dive into Premium Features

### Day 8: Breed ID in Action

*   **Instagram/TikTok (Reel):** A compilation of 3-4 different users discovering their dogs' breeds with the app. Show the surprise and delight on their faces. 
*   **Facebook:** Share a user's story in more detail. "We always thought she was a lab mix, but it turns out she's part boxer! It explains so much about her energy levels!"

### Day 9: Tip Tuesday - Medication Reminders

*   **Instagram (Carousel):** "How to use InterpWhat to create a medication reminder in 30 seconds." Show screenshots of the process.
*   **TikTok:** A quick video showing how to set up a recurring daily reminder for flea and tick medication.

### Day 10: Case Study - Understanding Sibling Rivalry

*   **Facebook:** A longer post about how a user used the Multi-Pet Comparison feature to identify triggers for conflict between their two cats. Include anonymized timeline graphs.
*   **Twitter:** A thread summarizing the case study with key takeaways.

### Day 11: Ask the Expert

*   **Instagram Stories (Q&A Sticker):** "Ask our vet consultant anything about breed-specific care!" Answer the top 5 questions in subsequent stories.

### Day 12: The Power of a Timeline

*   **Instagram/TikTok (Reel):** Show a pet's timeline, but this time, overlay it with reminder icons for vet visits, grooming, etc. Show how the timeline becomes a complete health record.

### Day 13: Sneak Peek

*   **All Platforms:** A graphic that says "Our BIGGEST deal of the year is coming. Get ready to upgrade your pet parenting game. #BlackFriday"

### Day 14: Community Spotlight

*   **Instagram/Facebook:** Share a post from a user who posted about their experience with the app (with their permission). Tag them and thank them for being part of the community.
